# GTA VI Cover Art Maker — make.fun entry

Turn your photo into a Vice City poster. An offline canvas poster app with photo upload, presets, panel layouts and PNG export. Nothing leaves your browser.

## Run

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no server, no network needed. Click "Use sample photo" or drop your own photo, pick a preset and layout, then "Export PNG".
